# Flow: contact (key tasks: email him, open his CV)

**Goal.** At the end of the walk, one clear next step.

**Before.** "Get in touch", the email address, two plain text links.

**After (Phase 3).**
1. A direct question (`site.json` `contact.heading`): "Looking for a forestry trainee or field worker?"
2. One line from the CV (`contact.text`): trainee, summer and field work; full-time; start immediately; travel anywhere in Finland.
3. The email address large (Fraunces).
4. **Email me** (the one primary button) · **Open my CV (PDF)** (outline) · GitHub (link).
5. Location in mono.
6. Footer: a signature: name · place, "Updated <month year>" (the build date), ©.

**States.** No CV configured: the CV button is left out. Typing `cv`, `contact`, `email`… in search flies here and focuses the heading.

**Heuristics.** 8 Minimalism (one question, two actions); 4 Consistency (same Email / CV pair as the header).

**Accessibility.** Heading is focusable (`tabindex="-1"`) for search and nav landings; buttons are links with verb-first labels (DESIGN.md §6.6).
